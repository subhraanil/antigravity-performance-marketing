# ApexLink High-Converting Outreach & Pitch Templates

Proven, battle-tested templates across 5 core link acquisition channels.

---

## 1. Digital PR & Journalist Pitch (Qwoted / HARO / Connectively / Featured)

### Template A: The "Data-Anchor" Hook (Highest Acceptance Rate)
**Subject:** `[Expert Insight] Research & Benchmarks for: {Journalist Request Topic}`
```text
Hi {Journalist_First_Name},

I saw your query regarding {topic} and wanted to share verified benchmarks from our recent research at {Company_Name}:

- Key Finding: {Specific_Stat_Or_Metric, e.g., "Over 68% of engineering teams report a 2.4x acceleration in deployment cycles after adopting automated pre-flight testing."}
- Trend Signal: {One sentence on why this is happening right now, e.g., "The underlying shift is driven by teams replacing legacy manual reviews with deterministic rule checks."}

We compiled the full breakdown of verified primary data and market benchmarks here for reference: {Linkable_Asset_URL}

Feel free to use the metric or quote directly in your story. If you need any follow-up commentary or an executive perspective, I am happy to reply quickly.

Best regards,

{Expert_Name}
{Expert_Title} | {Company_Name}
{LinkedIn_URL}
```

### Template B: The "Contrarian Perspective" Hook
**Subject:** `Counter-intuitive finding on {Topic} from {Company_Name}`
```text
Hi {Journalist_First_Name},

Most coverage around {Topic} focuses on {Conventional_Belief}. However, our direct operational data across {Customer_Cohort_Count} teams shows the exact opposite:

- The Reality: "{Contrarian_Quote, e.g., 'Companies spending 3x more on top-of-funnel traffic are experiencing a 40% lower downstream retention rate than those prioritizing onboarding speed.'}"

Why this happens: {1-2 sentence crisp explanation}.

Happy to unpack the data or jump on a 5-minute call if you need more context for your piece!

Best,

{Expert_Name}
{Expert_Title} | {Company_Name}
```

## 2. Competitor Broken Link Reclamation

**Subject:** `Broken link on {Site_Name}'s {Page_Title}`
```text
Hi {Editor_First_Name},

I was reading your comprehensive guide on {Topic} ({Target_Page_URL}) — fantastic breakdown, particularly the section on {Specific_Section_Name}.

While reviewing the resources, I noticed that the link pointing to {Competitor_Dead_URL} appears to be broken / returns a 404 error.

Our team at {Company_Name} recently published a freshly updated, research-backed guide on that exact workflow:
{Your_Replacement_URL}

If you are updating the page, it might make a helpful replacement for your readers. Either way, thanks for putting together such a valuable resource!

Cheers,

{Your_Name}
{Your_Title} | {Company_Name}
```

## 3. High-Value Resource Page Inclusion

**Subject:** `Resource suggestion for {Site_Name}'s {Page_Title}`
```text
Hi {Editor_First_Name},

I have been using your {Resource_Page_Name} ({Resource_Page_URL}) as a bookmark for our team's research.

I noticed you list several tools for {Category}, such as {Tool_A} and {Tool_B}.

We recently launched {Your_Product_Name} ({Your_Website_URL}) — a free/open-source {Brief_Description, e.g., "developer tool that automates API contract testing"}. It differs from existing options by {Unique_Differentiator, e.g., "running entirely locally in the browser with zero cloud telemetry"}.

If you feel it would add value to your readers, would you consider adding it to the list?

Thanks for your time and continued curation!

Best,

{Your_Name}
{Company_Name}
```

{Your_Name}

{Your_Name}
On behalf of {Founder_Name}
