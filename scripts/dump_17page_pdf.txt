2. Unit Economics, Checkout Optimization & Retention Strategy
To maintain complete financial clarity, this strategy articulates the exact contribution economics of customer acquisition. As an authorized 
distributor for professional salon brands (Swiss Beauty, L'Oréal Professionnel, Matrix, Schwarzkopf, Floractive), FABB6 operates on gross 
wholesale product margins of ~25% on ex-GST trade value. Assuming 18% GST, mathematical media break-even requires a 4.00x ROAS on ex-
GST revenue (1 ÷ 0.25) and 4.72x ROAS on GST-inclusive total receipts (4.00x × 1.18). Front-end paid media acquires customers near first-
order margin break-even, while sustainable profit is achieved through repeat replenishment that incurs zero incremental media cost.
Low-Cost Retention Channel Architecture (Zero Incremental Media Spend)
Repeat retention is driven primarily by automated WhatsApp and email replenishment workflows timed to hair spa, shampoo, and color 
usage cycles (30, 45, and 60 days). While retention software and messaging incurs operational costs (~₹35,000–₹50,000/month), it requires 
zero incremental media spend. This zero-media-cost retention engine unlocks 3,096 (Dec), 3,313 (Jan), and 3,545 (Feb) monthly 'Orders from

Merchant Center Product Sync Tool Recommendation: Simprosys Google Shopping Feed
To permanently eliminate wasted spend on 404 broken links (110 broken URLs identified in audit) and out-of-stock products, we recommend 
deploying Simprosys Google Shopping Feed (Shopify App):
• Real-Time Webhook Synchronization: Directly syncs Shopify inventory and price updates via Shopify webhooks to Google Content API in 
minutes. When an item drops to 0 inventory, it is instantly suppressed from Google Shopping and PMax ads, halting click bleed.
• Automated 404 Broken Link Detection: Continuously validates Shopify product URLs. Inactive, deleted, or draft products are 
automatically removed from the active feed before Merchant Center disapproves them.
• Automated Custom Label & Margin Tagging: Maps Shopify product tags (high_28plus, mid_20_28, EXCLUDE_lowmargin, single_sachet) 
to custom_label_0 through custom_label_4 for automated campaign bid gating.
• Low Cost & High ROI: Operational cost is ~$15–$25/month (~₹1,200–₹2,100/mo), paying for itself on day 1 by preventing broken URL 
waste. Enterprise alternative: Feedonomics for advanced multi-channel rule engines.

Three-Tier Scaling Decision Governance (Weekly Performance Gates)
Monthly budget scaling from December to January and February is strictly governed by trailing 7-day verified store performance:
• Green Light (Scale): Trailing 7-day confirmed Gross ROAS ≥ 2.50x AND blended CPA within monthly target (Dec ≤ ₹480, Jan ≤ ₹450, Feb ≤ 
₹410). Approve scheduled monthly budget increase, capped at ≤ 20% every 72 hours per campaign.
• Yellow Light (Hold): Trailing 7-day Gross ROAS between 2.30x and 2.50x. Hold daily budgets completely flat. Analyze SKU-level 
contribution, rotate creative fatigue, and allow Smart Bidding algorithms to consolidate.
• Red Light (Rollback): Trailing 7-day Gross ROAS < 2.30x for 7 consecutive days (or blended CPA exceeds target by >25%). Immediately 
roll back the last budget increase by 20% and pause any underperforming ad set.

• Gross Sales Revenue: Total confirmed sales revenue recorded in Shopify before cancellations, discounts and returns. Primary top-line

6. Performance Audit Report
6.1 Executive Summary of the Audit
This section reviews Google Ads and Meta Ads performance from December 2025 to 18 November 2026, audits the state of the Google Ads 
and Meta Ads accounts for the period September to November 2026, and sets out a stabilization plan and a forecast for December 2026 to 
February 2027.
What the numbers say
• In August 2026, FABB6 recorded ₹74.76 Lakhs of Total Sales Revenue across 11,069 orders on ₹47.91 Lakhs of ad spend (1.56x Total 
Sales ROAS).
• Between 1 September and 18 November 2026, ad spend was ₹67.83 Lakhs (Google ₹34.84L, Meta ₹32.99L) against Total Sales Revenue 
of ₹1.61 Cr.
• Headline efficiency in October (2.14x Total Sales ROAS) and November to date (2.20x) was higher than June to August (1.56x–1.96x). 
Paid CPA in October (₹582) was 33% above August (₹437), but AOV on Total Sales rose from ₹675 to ₹995 (+47%), compensating for the 
CPA rise.
• The most serious disruption was September: ad spend fell 87% against August (₹6.27L) and Shopify orders fell 72% to 3,112. The 
contraction stemmed from campaign pauses during onboarding and billing verification, compounded by tracking deletion.
What the audit found
The problems are in account hygiene, tracking and structure rather than in a collapse of headline efficiency. Each is documented in the 
change history and exports (Exhibits A–F):
• The primary Google purchase conversion action ('Purchase-2026') was deleted on 18 September.
• Live Performance Max asset groups contained copy referencing another brand.
• 110 products pointed to unavailable (404) pages in Merchant Center.
• Meta retargeting (DPA) ran more expensively than cold prospecting, three Advantage+ Shopping campaigns ran concurrently, and about 
₹1.5 Lakhs was spent on campaigns with zero orders.
Quantified waste: between ₹1.50 Lakhs (spend with zero orders, provable) and ₹7.47 Lakhs (total spend in the affected campaigns, upper 
bound). Our central estimate of excess spend is about ₹5.9 Lakhs (Section 6.5).
Forecast (Dec 2026 – Feb 2027): on a planned ad spend of ₹91.1 Lakhs, the forecast delivers about ₹3.56 Cr of Total Sales Revenue (3.91x 
blended ROAS) across 31,574 store orders (₹288.53 blended CPA), with conservative (3.00x / ₹2.73 Cr) and stretch (4.20x / ₹3.82 Cr) 
sensitivity scenarios. The forecast is built on Total Sales Revenue, the primary sales metric in the financial model (Section 8).

Three observations that shape the plan
• 1. Efficiency adjusted as spend scaled: From December 2025 to August 2026, spend grew more than tenfold and Total Sales ROAS 
adjusted from 4.92x to 1.56x. Some decline is the normal cost of scaling into colder audiences, and we treat 1.8x–2.5x as the realistic 
operating baseline for ₹25–45 Lakhs of monthly spend.
• 2. September's 4.54x is not an efficiency gain: Spend was cut by 87% due to campaign pauses and verification delays, so the ratio 
reflects the organic and repeat demand that continued without paid support.
• 3. October CPA was not a record: April (₹608) and June (₹626) were higher. October ROAS (2.14x) and November to date (2.20x) are 
well above August (1.56x), driven by stronger order values.
The Festival of Lights (Diwali) fell on 8 November in 2026, so the festive demand window straddles October and November. The November 
run-rate therefore includes festive uplift and is not used as an unadjusted December baseline.
6.3 Google Ads Audit
Google Ads spend during the audit period (1 September – 18 November 2026) totaled ₹34.84 Lakhs. Findings from the change history, asset 
group, product and search term reports:
• 1. Primary purchase conversion deleted (18 Sep): The change history shows the 'Purchase-2026' conversion action was deleted by an 
administrator account (Exhibit A). Smart Bidding lost its main learning signal. September Google spend was ₹3.18 Lakhs for 42 attributed 
orders (₹7,569 CPA). Because 17 days of the month preceded the deletion and post-deletion sales may be unattributed, the true cost per 
sale is likely lower than this figure, and we will validate it against Shopify UTM data.
• 2. Another brand's copy in live Performance Max asset groups: Several asset groups (T4 Pro/Salon, T3 Premium, Pilgrim, YS24) 
contained headlines and descriptions promoting a different brand name (Exhibit B). All were rated 'Poor' for Ad Strength.
• 3. 110 broken product pages: Merchant Center shows 216 ineligible products: 110 with 'page unavailable' (404) and 70 with 
promotional overlay violations. At least ₹80,285 was spent on affected products with zero conversions (for example, one serum SKU 
spent ₹1,614 on 480 clicks into a 404 page).
• 4. Frequent budget changes: Daily budgets were changed 140 times in under 60 days (74 increases, 66 decreases). We cannot isolate 
the effect on delivery, but constant changes make Smart Bidding performance harder to read and slower to stabilize.
• 5. Off-category search queries: A campaign named 'Search-Brand' used broad match and triggered queries unrelated to beauty (for 
example, gym equipment and a badminton ankle support). The spend on these queries is not yet quantified; it will be after a full search 
terms export.
6.4 Meta Ads Audit
Meta spend during the audit period (1 September – 18 November 2026) totaled ₹32.99 Lakhs.
• 1. Retargeting more expensive than prospecting: 'Retargeting — DPA - Fabb6' spent ₹9.50 Lakhs for 1,850 purchases at ₹513.70 CPA 
(peaking at ₹570.31 in October), against ₹479.53 for cold prospecting, with frequency of 3.21 and no past-purchaser exclusions. Excess 
cost against the cold-prospecting CPA is about ₹63,000.
• 2. Overlapping Advantage+ Shopping campaigns: Three Advantage+ Shopping campaigns ran at the same time without product 
exclusions, so they competed in the same auctions. Account CPM rose from ₹56.15 in August to ₹104.17 in October (+85%) and ₹137.03 
in November (+144%). Q4 festive demand raises CPMs for all advertisers, so part of this rise is seasonal. Consolidation to one master 
Advantage+ campaign will let us measure the effect cleanly.
• 3. 'IG-Traffic' link-click campaign: ₹50,214 across 8.32 million impressions produced 1,02,322 link clicks, 28 landing page views and 0 
purchases (99.97% drop-off between click and page view).
• 4. App install campaigns: The iOS campaign spent ₹19,161 at frequency 13.18 with 0 sales. The Android campaign spent about ₹1.38 
Lakhs at ₹1,082 CPA.
• 5. Click-to-landing-page gap: Meta reported 7,34,064 link clicks but only 3,01,798 landing page views on Shopify: a 58.9% gap (52.2% 
excluding IG-Traffic). Likely causes are mobile page speed, accidental clicks, and pixel or Conversions API (CAPI) misfires. We will 
diagnose these in the first two weeks.

7. Turnaround Plan
Admin access has been verified across Google Ads, Meta Business Suite, Google Merchant Center and Shopify. Shopify is the source of truth 
for revenue and orders throughout.
Phase 1: Immediate triage (hours 0 – 48)
• Pause 'IG-Traffic' and the iOS and Android app install campaigns.
• Remove foreign-brand copy from all Performance Max asset groups.
• Exclude the 110 unavailable (404) products in Merchant Center and fix or remove the overlay violations.
• Confirm a single primary Purchase conversion action is active in Google Ads and matches Shopify order counts. Verify Enhanced 
Conversions, and test Meta CAPI deduplication.
• Export search terms and add negative keywords for off-category queries.
Phase 2: Restructure and stabilize (December 2026)
• Segment Performance Max into Hero Best-Sellers (60% of budget), Long-Tail Catalog (20%) and Search/Brand (20%).
• Consolidate Meta into one master Advantage+ Shopping campaign with a 20% existing-customer budget cap.
• Add 30-day and 180-day purchaser exclusions to DPA, with frequency capped at 2.2 or below.
• Launch 10 new creator (UGC) assets focused on winter skincare routines and gifting.
• Diagnose the click-to-landing-page gap (page speed, pixel and CAPI audit).
Phase 3: Profitable scaling (January – February 2027)
• Increase budgets by no more than 20% every 72 hours per campaign.
• Expand high-intent non-brand Google search for hero categories (serums, brightening, anti-aging).
• Launch post-purchase replenishment flows (email and WhatsApp) at 30, 45 and 60 days. Retention revenue is treated as upside and is 
not included in the forecast.
Scaling decision rule: scale a step only if trailing seven-day Total Sales ROAS is at least 2.4x and CPA is within target. Hold spend between 2.2x 
and 2.4x. Roll back the last increase if the ratio stays below 2.2x for seven days. All planned budget expansions in Section 8 are strictly 
conditional on maintaining this performance threshold.

9. Operating Guardrails
• 1. Paused-first creation: New campaigns, ad sets and creatives are created paused and go live only after tracking, UTM parameters and 
margin review are confirmed.
• 2. Budget pacing: Increases are capped at 20% every 72 hours per campaign.
• 3. Audience hygiene: Prospecting campaigns exclude 30-day purchasers and customer lists. Retargeting excludes purchasers.

Shopify net revenue.

12. Conversion Rate Optimization
12.1 Why it matters for this plan
Every month of the forecast assumes a higher Return On Ad Spend than the month before. Three levers can deliver that without raising cost 
per click: a higher share of visitors who become buyers, a higher order value, and more repeat purchases. This section works on the first two, 
and on the checkout step that connects them. The audit points to the same place:
• Only 41.1% of Meta link clicks became Shopify landing page views (a 58.9% gap, Section 6.4), so visitors are being lost before the page 
even loads.
