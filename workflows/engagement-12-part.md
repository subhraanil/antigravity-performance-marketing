# 12-Part Digital Marketing Strategy Engagement Workflow
# Source: digital-marketing-pro framework

Execute the canonical 12-part strategy flow to audit, position, and scale any brand.

## Phase 1: Intake & Market Research (Unbiased)
1. **Part 1 — Client Intake (Stone vs. Opinion)**:
   - Separate verifiable facts (Stone: past revenue, verified CPA, margin data, active geographies) from assumptions (Opinion: "Our audience loves X", "Competitor Y is cheaper").
2. **Part 2 — External Market Research**:
   - Independent market sizing, industry benchmark queries, macro trends without client preconceptions.

## Phase 2: Core Strategic Foundation
3. **Part 3 — Four Core Documents (61 Steps)**:
   - 3.1 Business & SBU Analysis (Unit economics, margins, LTV/CAC).
   - 3.2 Segmentation Framework (Actionable persona definitions, TAM/SAM/SOM).
   - 3.3 Brand Positioning & Communications (Differentiation statement, tone of voice, proof points).
   - 3.4 DMFlow (Digital Marketing Flow, customer journey mapping).
4. **Part 4 — Competitive & Audience Deep Dive**:
   - Competitor ad monitoring (Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency), keyword share of voice, gap analysis.

## Phase 3: Validation & Recalibration
5. **Part 5 — Client Validation Gate**:
   - Deliver Client Validation Document; client marks findings as Accept / Reject / Edit.
6. **Part 6 — Selective v2 Re-runs**:
   - Apply Decision Matrix to re-run only invalidated downstream documents. Maintain v1 (unbiased market reality) and v2 (client-reconciled operating reality).

## Phase 4: Operating Architecture & Execution
7. **Part 7 — Preparation Layer**:
   - Campaign architecture, KPI tree, content pillars, approval matrices.
8. **Part 8 — Growth Plan & 12-Month Yearly Planner**:
   - Flagship 11-section strategy document, channel budget splits, milestone timeline.
9. **Part 9 — Channel Strategy Fan-Out**:
   - Detailed plans for Paid Social (Meta, TikTok), Paid Search (Google), SEO & AEO, Lifecycle Email, and Influencers.
10. **Part 10 — Execution Artefacts**:
    - High-converting ad copy (Hook-Hold-Offer), RSA copy matrices, email sequences, landing page briefs.
11. **Part 11 — Creative Briefs (AI & Human Production)**:
    - Visual storyboards, EU AI Act Article 50 & C2PA disclosure tags, model specs (Gemini Omni, Imagen 3, Veo 3.1).
12. **Part 12 — Continuous Improvement Loop**:
    - Quarterly retrospective, signal feed-back into product offering and pricing strategy.
