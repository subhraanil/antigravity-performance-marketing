# Continuous Improvement & Quarterly Rebalancing Loop

Quarterly feedback loop connecting real-world media performance back into positioning, creative production, and product offering adjustments.

---

## 1. Quarterly Performance Audit (QBR Preparation)
1. Aggregate 90 days of performance data across:
   - In-platform reported metrics (Spend, CPA, ROAS, Conversions).
   - First-party business metrics (Total Revenue, Blended MER, Contribution Margin, Net New Customers).
2. Calculate Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER):
   $$\text{MER} = \frac{\text{Total Business Revenue}}{\text{Total Paid Ad Spend}}$$
3. Identify top 10% highest-value customer acquisition channels and ad creatives.

---

## 2. Creative Angle & Customer Feedback Analysis
1. Analyze comment sections, customer support tickets, and post-purchase surveys.
2. Note:
   - What objections recur most frequently? (Price, shipping time, ingredients, complexity).
   - What unexpected use-cases or customer benefits are cited?
3. Feed these discoveries into the next quarter's 3x3 Creative Testing Matrix.

---

## 3. Channel Rebalancing (70/20/10 Reallocation)
- Evaluate each channel's marginal ROAS and saturation curve.
- Shift budget away from channels experiencing severe diminishing returns.
- Rebalance the 70% Core Scaled budget into highest-incrementality platforms.
- Allocate fresh 10% Moonshot budget to test emerging ad platforms (e.g. TikTok Shop, YouTube Shorts, Reddit Ads).

---

## 4. Brand Profile & Strategy Document Versioning (Update-Back Rule)
- Update `brands/{client_slug}.json` with new historical benchmarks and updated target CPAs.
- Update Living Project Instruction File to propagate learned restrictions and successful messaging hooks to all subagents.
