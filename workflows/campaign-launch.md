# Campaign Launch Workflow & Pre-Flight Checklist

Ensure zero-defect deployment across Meta Ads and Google Ads.

## Pre-Flight Checklist
- [ ] **Creation Status**: Verify campaign is generated in `PAUSED` state.
- [ ] **Budget Cap**: Confirm initial daily budget is within approved bounds (≤ 20% scale cap rule).
- [ ] **Exclusions Active**: Exclude 30d purchasers and customer lists in prospecting.
- [ ] **UTM Discipline**: Complete GA4 tagging on all destination URLs.
- [ ] **Tracking Health**: Verify Meta Pixel + CAPI test events firing; verify Google Enhanced Conversions tag is active.
- [ ] **Asset Quality**: Visuals adhere to 9:16 (Reels/Stories) and 1:1/4:5 feed formats.
- [ ] **Compliance Gate**: C2PA metadata embedded; AI disclosure present if synthetic avatars/voice used.
- [ ] **Human Sign-off**: Explicit typed confirmation received from human strategist.
