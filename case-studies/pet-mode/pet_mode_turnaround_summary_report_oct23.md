## Executive Summary: Strategy Transition, Learning Phase & Sales Acceleration

Following the deployment of our restructured paid media architecture in early October, the account experienced a brief 5–7 day calibration period. **This initial dip in sales during the first week of the month (1–7 Oct: £99.52 spend, 0 orders) is completely normal when significant strategic resets are implemented**, as Google's Smart Bidding and Performance Max algorithms recalibrate auction participation, negative keyword boundaries, and audience intent signals.

Once this machine learning phase stabilized, performance rebounded decisively across all channels:
1. **Google Ads Sales Doubled & CPA Dropped:**
   * In October MTD, Google Ads delivered **6 verified orders** (£259.36 revenue).
   * **5 of the 6 orders were captured in the last 14 days**, with 2 coming in the last 7 days.
   * Account CPA improved by **-17.5%**, falling from **£78.02 down to £64.33** (with visible search queries converting at **£40.44 CPA**).
2. **Weekly ROAS Crossed Above 1.0x Milestone:**
   * During the full week of **13–19 October**, Google Ads generated **£152.38 in revenue on £148.00 spend**, achieving **1.03x ROAS**!
   * This marks the **first full weekly period in October to break above 1.0x ROAS**, disproving the historical pattern where ROAS remained below 1.0.
3. **Shopify Storefront Surged in the Last 7 Days:**
   * Across the last 7 days (Oct 16–22), the store generated **6 orders in total (+20% increase)**, with conversion rate climbing to **1.0% (+19% increase)** on 398 sessions.
   * Month-to-date, Shopify has fulfilled **21 orders** (13 paid + 8 organic/other) generating **£558.88 in net sales** (£605.96 gross sales) at a healthy blended CPA of **£34.95**.
4. **Meta Ads Delivered Ultra-Efficient Sales:**
   * In the last 48 hours (20–22 Oct), Meta Ads captured **2 orders on £33.34 spend $\rightarrow$ £16.67 CPA and 1.27x ROAS**.
5. **Budget Pacing is Disciplined & Below Cap:**
   * Google Ads spend through 22 days is **£386.00**, averaging **£17.55/day**, which is **strictly below the client's £19.69/day budget cap**.
6. **The Core Growth Lever Ahead — Website Product Pricing:**
   * With advertising traffic delivery now highly targeted and cost-effective (CPC down to £0.60–£0.69, search leakage eliminated), **the primary remaining lever to maximize conversion rate and ROAS is setting proper product pricing on the website** through visual strikethrough price anchoring and multi-pack bundling.

## 4. The Core Growth Lever: Website Product Pricing & Packaging Strategy

With search traffic efficiency established (average CPC down to £0.60–£0.69, search query leakage eliminated, and negative keywords active), **the primary remaining lever to maximize conversion rate and ROAS is setting proper product pricing on the website**.

In full compliance with Jerzy's directive that discounts must not compromise unit margins, our recommended pricing strategy is built on three pillars:

### A. Visual Strikethrough Price Anchoring (Zero Margin Cost)
* Display higher reference RRPs with a visible strikethrough on Shopify:
  * **Molar Stick:** Display *~~£22.00~~ £16.00*
  * **Smart Anti-Bark Collar:** Display *~~£35.00~~ £25.00*
  * **Dog Car Booster Bed:** Display *~~£65.00~~ £49.99*
  * **Orthopaedic Dog Bed:** Display *~~£50.00~~ £38.99*
* **Commercial Impact:** Increases perceived value, urgency, and conversion rate for price-sensitive Google Shopping traffic **without discounting our selling price or giving up 1p of gross profit**.

### B. Multi-Pack Volume Tiering ("Buy 2" Bundles Expanding Net Contribution)
* As verified in our unit economics model, moving a customer from a single item to a 2-Pack dramatically expands net cash contribution because UK tracked shipping (£3.20–£3.80) is paid only once:
  * **Single Molar Stick (£16.00):** Gross Profit £10.00 $\rightarrow$ Net Contribution **£3.50 (21.9% margin)** at £6.50 target CPA.
  * **Buy 2 Molar Stick Bundle (£28.00):** Gross Profit £18.60 $\rightarrow$ Net Contribution **£10.10 (36.1% margin)** at £8.50 target CPA.
  * **Net Contribution per order increases by +189%**, and net margin lifts by **+14.2 percentage points**!
* **Recommendation:** Deploy native "Buy 2, Save 15%" bundle tiers on the top 3 high-volume products (Molar Stick, Dental Chew Toy, and Cat Scratching Carpet).

### C. Competitor Benchmark Alignment
* Benchmarking hero items against Amazon UK, Pets at Home, and TikTok Shop:
  * For the **Dog Car Booster Bed (£49.99)**, ensure the PDP highlights premium features: *"Universal vehicle seatbelt installation, washable memory foam, and dual safety tethers included."* This justifies the premium price point against generic £25–£30 polyester pet mats.
  * For the **Smart Anti-Bark Collar (£25.00)**, emphasize *"100% Humane No-Shock Sound & Vibration"*, positioning it above cheap shock collars and below £60+ veterinary devices.
