## Phase 1: Foundation & Account Setup (Technical Prerequisites)

In this phase, we establish the technical foundation. All software licenses, domains, and ad accounts are registered in your name so that you retain 100% ownership and direct billing control.

### 1. Dedicated Gmail Account
* **Purpose:** Acts as the central administrative hub for your Google Ads account, Google Analytics, YouTube channel access, and project communication. Keeping this separate from personal email ensures clean asset handover and team sharing.
* **Requirements:**
  * Create a new Gmail address (e.g., `ammaalochana.ads@gmail.com` or `ammaalochana.marketing@gmail.com`).
  * Ensure 2-factor authentication is configured with your recovery phone number.
* **Client Action:** Share the email address and temporary login access with our team to configure the ad platform.
* **Status:** `[ ] Pending`

---

### 2. Domain Purchase (For High-Converting Landing Page)
* **Purpose:** A dedicated, clean domain (or subdomain) is required to host your masterclass registration page. Custom domains build visitor trust and are mandatory for Meta domain verification and tracking.
* **Recommended Registrars:** GoDaddy, Hostinger, or Namecheap.
* **Suggested Domain Names:**
  * Option A: `ammaalochana.com` or `ammaalochanatrading.com`
  * Option B (if you already own a primary website): We can create a dedicated subdomain, e.g., `webinar.ammaalochana.com` or `event.ammaalochana.com`.
* **Client Action:** Purchase the domain and provide registrar panel access (or delegate DNS management) so we can map SSL certificates and landing page CNAME records.
* **Status:** `[ ] Pending`

---

### 3. CRM for Automation & Landing Page (GoHighLevel or Systeme.io)
* **Purpose:** The core engine of your lead generation system. It hosts the fast-loading mobile registration page, collects participant details, sends instant WhatsApp & Email confirmations, and triggers automated reminders on the day of the live session.
* **Recommended Options:**
  * **Option A: GoHighLevel (GHL)** — Industry standard for advanced WhatsApp Cloud API integrations, custom calendars, and high-volume lead management.
  * **Option B: Systeme.io** — Lightweight, cost-effective all-in-one platform for fast landing pages and email workflows.
* **Client Action:** Purchase a monthly subscription to your chosen tool and invite our email as an **Admin / Developer** to begin building the funnel.
* **Status:** `[ ] Pending`

### 4. Meta Ads & Social Assets Access
* **Purpose:** Allows us to create and manage targeted lead generation campaigns across Instagram and Facebook using Vara Prasad's official profile.
* **Access Items Required:**
  1. **Facebook Business Page:** Admin/Advertiser access to the official Amma Alochana page.
  2. **Instagram Account:** Access to [@varaprasad_46](https://www.instagram.com/varaprasad_46/?hl=en) (must be connected as a Professional/Creator account to the Facebook Page).

* **Status:** `[ ] Pending`

## Phase 2: Content Creation & Webinar Configuration

Once the technical foundation is active, we move into content and event scheduling.

### 1. Video Content Shooting (Client Role)
* **Our Responsibility:**
  * We provide 2 to 3 proven, high-converting Telugu ad scripts with exact timestamps (`[0:00–0:05] Hook`, `[0:05–0:20] Problem`, `[0:20–0:35] Solution`, `[0:35–0:45] Call to Action`).
  * We provide clear filming instructions: smartphone camera settings (4K/1080p @ 60fps), vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, eye-level framing, lighting, and audio clarity.
* **Client Responsibility:**
  * Shoot 2 to 3 raw video takes speaking directly into the smartphone camera.
  * Upload the raw unedited `.mp4` or `.mov` files directly to our shared Google Drive folder.
* **Our Post-Production:**
  * We handle all video editing, pacing, dynamic on-screen Telugu & English subtitles, hook animations, audio cleaning, and compliance checks (SEBI educational disclaimers).
* **Status:** `[ ] Pending`

---

### 2. Zoom Webinar Link & Event Schedule
* **Purpose:** To configure automated 1-click Google/Apple calendar invites, instant WhatsApp confirmations, and day-of reminder messages, the exact event schedule must be pre-programmed into the CRM.
* **Requirements:**
  * **Zoom Pro / Webinar Account:** Ensure the Zoom account supports 100+ live participants with no 40-minute cutoff limit.
  * **Event Details:**
    * Confirmed Date & Time (e.g., Sunday, 7:30 PM – 8:30 PM IST).
    * Webinar Topic / Title.
    * Recurring / Static Zoom Join Link + Passcode.
* **Client Action:** Generate the Zoom meeting link and share the URL + Passcode with our team.
* **Status:** `[ ] Pending`
