## Guidelines for All Copy
- **Tone:** Honest, relatable, problem-solution. Never clinical or over-salesy.
- **Language:** English with light Indian relatability cues. Avoid direct medical claims.
- **Emoji usage:** 1–2 max per post. Used for emphasis, not decoration.
- **CTA:** Always "Shop Now" for purchase campaigns. "Learn More" for TOFU only.
- **Character limits:** Primary text: 125 chars (above fold). Headline: 40 chars max.
