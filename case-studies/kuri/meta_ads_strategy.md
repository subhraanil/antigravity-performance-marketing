## 1. Executive Summary — 4 Core Issues We Are Solving

> [!WARNING]
> **1. Low Social Proof & Weak Brand Presence**
> - **Current:** 13 Facebook followers, 1,060 Instagram followers, near-zero organic reach.
> - **Problem:** Cold ad traffic sees 13 followers on Facebook and hesitates to purchase.
> - **Fix:** Launch TOFU campaigns immediately (10% budget = ₹7,500/mo) to build FB page likes and seed video retargeting pools.

> [!WARNING]
> **2. Low Customer Repeat Rate (Only 5.3% All-Time)**
> - **Current:** Only 94 repeat buyers out of 1,775 total purchasers (August had only 4 repeat buyers = 1.3%).
> - **Problem:** 95%+ of revenue relies on expensive new customer acquisition.
> - **Fix:** Re-engage all 1,803 past customers via dedicated retention ads (in Remarketing campaign), plus automated WhatsApp/Email flows.

> [!WARNING]
> **3. Past Meta Strategy = Only Fragmented Sales Ads (No Funnel)**
> - **Problem:** Past campaigns launched random sales ads without building awareness or retargeting warm audiences.
> - **Pixel Opportunity:** August recorded 2,400 Add-to-Carts and 1,000 Initiate Checkouts with ZERO follow-up ads.
> - **Fix:** Establish a structured 3-Part Funnel: **TOFU (10%)** → **MOFU New Acquisition (70%)** → **BOFU Remarketing & Retention (20%)**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **4. New Customer Acquisition Focus: Top 2 Bestsellers Only**
> - **Bestseller #1 (Blueberry Lip Treatment Oil):** Primary Hero SKU (~60% of Acquisition Budget = ₹31,500/mo).
> - **Bestseller #2 (Cherry Lip Treatment Oil):** Secondary Winner SKU (~40% of Acquisition Budget = ₹21,000/mo).
> - **Decision:** Zero cold acquisition budget will be wasted testing unproven or slower-converting SKUs.
