## 3. Why Forecast - B is More Realistic than Forecast - A

While Forecast - A created a good framework, it contained three fundamental operational vulnerabilities:

### 1. October Google Ads Underestimation in Forecast - A
* In **Forecast - A**, October Google Ads assumed CPC jumps to **£0.75** (from £0.46 in September) with only **2.0% Conv. Rate**, producing a **CPA of £37.50** and **0.72x ROAS** (operating at an artificial loss).
* In **Forecast - B**, Google Ads CPC is set at **£0.60** (realistic step-up from £0.46) with **2.4% Conv. Rate** (backed by recent weekly data where Google achieved 3.19% CR and £23.68 CPA). This makes Google Ads profitable right away in October (**CPA £25.00, ROAS 1.08x**).

### 2. December Meta Ads Overestimation in Forecast - A
* In **Forecast - A**, December Meta Ads assumed CPC drops to **£0.50** and Conv. Rate jumps to **4.0%**, yielding a **£12.50 CPA** and generating 64 orders (£1,920 revenue at 2.40x ROAS).
* **The Reality Check**:
  1. In the UK, December digital ad auctions have intense CPM competition across Meta. CPC will **not** collapse to £0.50 while spend increases to £800. A realistic December CPC is **£0.65**.
  2. Final Christmas shipping deadlines in the UK occur between **December 18 and 20** (Royal Mail / DPD cutoffs). Order volume naturally tapers off in the final 10 days of December. 115 store orders in December over-projects physical fulfillment reality.
* In **Forecast - B**, December is calibrated to **£0.65 CPC**, **3.6% Conv. Rate**, and **£18.06 CPA**, producing a solid **44.3 Meta orders** and **87.3 total store orders** at **1.84x ROAS**.

### 3. November (Black Friday / Cyber Monday) Under-leveraged in Forecast - A
* November is the single largest e-commerce shopping event of the year in the UK.
* In **Forecast - B**, Black Friday conversion surges are accurately captured: Meta Conv. Rate reaches **3.80%** (holiday impulse buying) with AOV at **£28.00**, delivering **80.4 total orders** and **£2,252.10 in revenue** at **1.73x ROAS**.
