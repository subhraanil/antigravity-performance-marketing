## 2. Unit Economics, Checkout Optimization & Retention Strategy

To maintain complete financial clarity, this strategy articulates the exact contribution economics of customer acquisition. As an authorized distributor for professional salon brands (Swiss Beauty, L'Oréal Professionnel, Matrix, Schwarzkopf, Floractive), FABB6 operates on gross wholesale product margins of ~25% on ex-GST trade value. Assuming 18% GST, mathematical media break-even requires a **4.00x ROAS on ex-GST revenue** ($1 \div 0.25$) and **4.72x ROAS on GST-inclusive total receipts** ($4.00\text{x} \times 1.18$). Front-end paid media acquires customers near first-order margin break-even, while sustainable profit is achieved through repeat replenishment that incurs zero incremental media cost.

### Low-Cost Retention Channel Architecture (Zero Incremental Media Spend)

Repeat retention is driven primarily by automated WhatsApp and email replenishment workflows timed to hair spa, shampoo, and color usage cycles (30, 45, and 60 days). While retention software and messaging incurs operational costs (~₹35,000–₹50,000/month), it requires **zero incremental media spend**. This zero-media-cost retention engine unlocks **3,096 (Dec), 3,313 (Jan), and 3,545 (Feb)** monthly 'Orders from Others' in the Recommended Plan:

| Workflow / Trigger Stage | Timing Window | Primary Messaging Channel | Target Product Category | Replenishment Trigger & Offer Architecture |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **W1: Core Shampoo & Conditioner** | Day 25 – 35 | WhatsApp Interactive Flow + Email | L'Oréal, Matrix, Schwarzkopf Care | Automated usage check-in; one-click re-order with 5% replenishment loyalty discount. |
| **W2: Hair Spa & Intensive Masques** | Day 40 – 50 | WhatsApp Catalog + RCS Message | L'Oréal Deep Nourish, Matrix Masque | Reminder that 500g tubs reach half-volume; salon combo upsell (Serum + Masque). |
| **W3: Salon Pro & Chemical Treatments** | Day 60 – 75 | Email VIP Concierge + WhatsApp | Floractive Nanoplastia, Raaga De-Tan | Professional salon replenishment cadence; bulk volume tier pricing for repeat buyers. |
| **W4: VIP Retention & Cross-Sell** | Day 90+ | Automated SMS / WhatsApp Template | Cross-Category Beauty / Skincare | Exclusive early access to seasonal combo kits; dynamic store credit balance alerts. |

### Required Technology Stack: GoKwik Checkout & Customer Notification Engine
* **GoKwik Checkout & Abandoned Cart Remarketing:** We will integrate **GoKwik** ([https://www.gokwik.co/](https://www.gokwik.co/)) on Shopify to maximize checkout conversion and remarketing:
  1. *1-Click KwikCheckout* with 100M+ pre-filled Indian shopper addresses, drastically reducing checkout abandonment.
  2. *Automated WhatsApp and SMS abandoned cart remarketing sequences* with personalized checkout recovery links, recapturing 15–20% of lost carts.
  3. *AI RTO risk intelligence and COD-to-Prepaid conversion discounts* to minimize failed delivery costs.
* **Existing Customer Notification Subscriptions:** To execute the W1–W4 replenishment flows, we deploy a dedicated WhatsApp Business API & automated notification subscription (such as Interakt, Wati, or Bik.ai) paired with Klaviyo email CRM. This infrastructure triggers timely replenishment notifications directly to existing customer phones based on purchase history, driving high repeat volume without any media spend.

### Merchant Center Product Sync Tool Recommendation: Simprosys Google Shopping Feed
To permanently eliminate wasted spend on 404 broken links (110 broken URLs identified in audit) and out-of-stock products, we recommend deploying **Simprosys Google Shopping Feed** (Shopify App):
* **Real-Time Webhook Synchronization:** Directly syncs Shopify inventory and price updates via Shopify webhooks to Google Content API in minutes. When an item drops to 0 inventory, it is instantly suppressed from Google Shopping and PMax ads, halting click bleed.
* **Automated 404 Broken Link Detection:** Continuously validates Shopify product URLs. Inactive, deleted, or draft products are automatically removed from the active feed before Merchant Center disapproves them.
* **Automated Custom Label & Margin Tagging:** Maps Shopify product tags (`high_28plus`, `mid_20_28`, `EXCLUDE_lowmargin`, `single_sachet`) to `custom_label_0` through `custom_label_4` for automated campaign bid gating.
* **Low Cost & High ROI:** Operational cost is ~$15–$25/month (~₹1,200–₹2,100/mo), paying for itself on day 1 by preventing broken URL waste. Enterprise alternative: Feedonomics.

### Three-Tier Scaling Decision Governance (Weekly Performance Gates)
Monthly budget scaling from December to January and February is strictly governed by trailing 7-day verified store performance:
* **Green Light (Scale):** Trailing 7-day confirmed Gross ROAS ≥ 2.50x AND blended CPA within monthly target (Dec ≤ ₹480, Jan ≤ ₹450, Feb ≤ ₹410). Approve scheduled monthly budget increase, capped at ≤ 20% every 72 hours per campaign.
* **Yellow Light (Hold):** Trailing 7-day Gross ROAS between 2.30x and 2.50x. Hold daily budgets completely flat. Analyze SKU-level contribution, rotate creative fatigue, and allow Smart Bidding algorithms to consolidate.
* **Red Light (Rollback):** Trailing 7-day Gross ROAS < 2.30x for 7 consecutive days (or blended CPA exceeds target by >25%). Immediately roll back the last budget increase by 20% and pause any underperforming ad set.

### Operational Measurement Definitions & KPI Hierarchy
* **Gross Sales Revenue:** Total confirmed sales revenue recorded in Shopify before cancellations, discounts and returns. Primary top-line metric evaluated weekly.
* **Shopify Gross ROAS:** Total Gross Sales Revenue divided by total ad spend. Account targets: 3.10x Dec, 3.46x Jan, 4.11x Feb.
* **Spend per Store Order (Blended CPA):** Total media spend divided by total Shopify store orders (₹326.87 Dec, ₹318.40 Jan, ₹299.72 Feb). Primary governing business unit metric.
* **Platform Paid CPA:** Ad spend divided by platform-reported orders (Google ₹480 → ₹407, Meta ₹518 → ₹410). Operational secondary guardrail.
* **Bidding CPA Ceilings:** Target cost ceilings entered in campaign bid settings (Search tCPA ₹407–₹480, Meta Cost Cap ₹410–₹518).
* **Agency Cadence:** Daily tracking snapshot via Slack/WhatsApp, weekly gate reviews, and monthly Shopify financial reconciliation.
