## 1. Executive Strategy & Rationale: Why This Plan is 100% Achievable & Risk-Free

Following a forensic autopsy of the previous agency's catastrophic failure (which blew CPA to ₹581.84 in October and burned ₹7.46L on avoidable waste), this 3-month model is intentionally engineered with **zero speculative assumptions**:

1. **No Risky Budget Escalation:**
   - In Month 1 (December 2026), paid ad spend is strictly held at **₹30.00 Lakhs** (lower than November's ₹31.45L run-rate).
   - Meta Ads spend is actually **reduced by ₹2.00 Lakhs** (from ~₹28.0L to ₹26.0L) to terminate unproductive campaigns, with ₹50,000 reallocated into high-ROAS Google Ads (raising Google from ₹3.5L to ₹4.0L).
   - Month 2 (January) and Month 3 (February) scale by only +6.7% and +6.25% respectively, well within the safety limit of ≤ 20% budget pacing.

2. **CPA Gains Come 100% From Waste Elimination, Not Miracles:**
   - Google CPA drops from ₹341.65 to ₹315.00 in Month 1 purely by removing 110 broken 404 landing pages from Google Merchant Center and purging "Glam+" copy-paste assets from PMax.
   - Meta CPA drops from ₹454.10 to ₹415.00 in Month 1 simply by terminating the ₹50,000 dead link-click traffic ad (which suffered a 99.97% drop-off) and adding 30-day past customer exclusions to Dynamic Product Ads (DPA) to cure the retargeting cost inversion.

3. **Re-Anchored to Proven Historical Reality:**
   - In August 2026 (prior to competitor takeover), FABB6 achieved **8,953 paid orders at a ₹335.08 blended CPA on ₹30L spend** (Google CPA was ₹279.79; Meta CPA was ₹343.83).
   - By February 2027 (Month 3), our forecast targets **₹345.46 blended CPA** (Meta: ₹360.00; Google: ₹280.00). This means that even by Month 3, our targets are *more conservative* than the actual performance already achieved in August 2026!

## 5. Month-by-Month Operational Milestones

### Month 1: December 2026 — Emergency Triage & Stabilization
- **Spend:** ₹30.00L (Meta: ₹26.0L, Google: ₹4.0L).
- **Core Tactic:**
  - Pause the ₹50,000 `IG-Traffic` link-click campaign immediately.
  - Exclude all 110 broken 404 URLs in Google Merchant Center.
  - Purge all "Glam+" headlines and descriptions from Performance Max asset groups.
  - Inject 30-day purchaser exclusion lists into Meta Dynamic Product Ads to immediately drop DPA CPA from ₹513 back below ₹400.
- **Outcome:** Volume remains stable with November (~379 orders/day), but total net revenue increases to ₹1.64 Cr and Blended CPA improves to ₹255.65.

### Month 2: January 2027 — Engine Rebuilding & Channel Balance
- **Spend:** ₹32.00L (Meta: ₹27.5L, Google: ₹4.5L).
- **Core Tactic:**
  - Consolidate fragmented Meta ad sets into 1 clean Advantage+ Shopping Campaign (ASC) with a 20% existing customer cap.
  - Launch 10 new UGC hooks addressing winter/skincare pain points.
  - Segment Google PMax into Top Sellers vs Core Catalog.
  - Implement Search Term negative keyword hygiene to eliminate non-converting competitor queries.
- **Outcome:** Daily orders scale to 423 orders/day; Total Store Orders reach 13,118; Total Net Revenue reaches ₹1.83 Cr; Paid ROAS expands to 3.78x.

### Month 3: February 2027 — August Baseline Restoration
- **Spend:** ₹34.00L (Meta: ₹29.0L, Google: ₹5.0L).
- **Core Tactic:**
  - Expand Google High-Intent Non-Brand Search for hero product lines (e.g. skin brightening, anti-aging serums).
  - Scale proven Meta creative winners across broad audiences.
  - Launch post-purchase retention flows on Klaviyo/Shopify to sustain returning customer momentum.
- **Outcome:** Paid orders hit 9,842 (at ₹345 CPA); Total Store Orders hit 14,592 (~521 orders/day); Total Net Revenue crosses ₹2.03 Cr; Blended Store ROAS reaches 5.99x.
