## 1. Executive Summary

This report reviews Google Ads and Meta Ads performance from December 2025 to 18 November 2026, audits the account state we inherited from the interim agency (SilverXis, September to November 2026), and sets out a stabilization plan and a forecast for December 2026 to February 2027.

### What the numbers say
* **August 2026 Baseline:** In August 2026, FABB6 recorded ₹95.80 Lakhs of Gross Sales and ₹74.76 Lakhs of Total Sales Revenue across 11,069 orders on ₹47.91 Lakhs of ad spend (2.00x Gross ROAS / 1.56x Net ROAS).
* **Interim Period Performance:** Between 1 September and 18 November 2026, ad spend was ₹67.83 Lakhs (Google ₹34.84L, Meta ₹32.99L) against Total Sales Revenue of ₹1.61 Cr and Gross Sales of ₹1.97 Cr.
* **Efficiency Realities:** October Gross ROAS (2.49x) was in line with June–July (2.51x–2.37x) and well above August (2.00x), while November to date (2.79x) reflects early festive uplift driven by stronger order values (October gross AOV was ₹1,156; November gross run-rate is ₹1,147).
* **The Core Breakdown:** The most serious disruption occurred in September: ad spend fell 87% against August (to ₹6.27L) and Shopify orders fell 72% to 3,112. Cause under review; change logs show campaign delivery reductions alongside the September 18 tracking deletion.

### What the audit found
The problems are in account hygiene, tracking and structure rather than in a collapse of headline efficiency. Each is documented in the change history and exports (Exhibits A–G):
* The primary Google purchase conversion action ('Purchase-2026') was deleted on 18 September.
* Live Performance Max asset groups contained copy referencing another brand.
* 110 products pointed to unavailable (404) pages in Merchant Center.
* Meta retargeting (DPA) ran more expensively than cold prospecting, three Advantage+ Shopping campaigns ran concurrently, and about ₹1.5 Lakhs was spent on campaigns with zero orders.

**Quantified waste:** between ₹1.50 Lakhs (spend with zero orders, provable) and ₹7.47 Lakhs (total spend in the affected campaigns, upper bound). Our central estimate of excess spend is about ₹5.87 Lakhs (Section 5).

**Forecast (Dec 2026 – Feb 2027):** The Recommended Plan paces media spend from ₹30.00L in December to ₹35.00L in January and ₹40.00L in February, delivering confirmed Gross Sales of ₹93.06L (3.10x ROAS), ₹121.25L (3.46x ROAS), and ₹164.42L (4.11x ROAS) across 9,178, 10,992, and 13,346 monthly store orders. The plan is powered by front-end paid acquisition (6,082, 7,680, and 9,801 monthly orders) and low-cost retention flows (3,096, 3,313, and 3,545 monthly orders) driven by GoKwik and automated WhatsApp/email replenishment workflows at zero incremental media spend. Flanked by a Moderate Plan (Dec ₹35L, Jan ₹41L, Feb ₹47L) and an Aggressive Plan (Dec ₹40L, Jan ₹48L, Feb ₹55L).

## 3. Google Ads Audit & Merchant Center Automation

Google Ads spend during the interim period totaled ₹34.84 Lakhs. Findings from the change history, asset group, product and search term reports:
* **1. Primary purchase conversion deleted (18 Sep):** The change history shows the 'Purchase-2026' conversion action was deleted by an administrator account on the SilverXis side (Exhibit A). Smart Bidding lost its main learning signal. September Google spend was ₹3.18 Lakhs for 42 attributed orders (₹7,569 CPA). Because 17 days of the month preceded the deletion and post-deletion sales may be unattributed, the true cost per sale is likely lower than this figure, and we will validate it against Shopify UTM data.
* **2. Another brand's copy in live Performance Max asset groups:** Several asset groups (T4 Pro/Salon, T3 Premium, Pilgrim, YS24) contained headlines and descriptions promoting a different brand name (Exhibit B). All were rated 'Poor' for Ad Strength.
* **3. 110 broken product pages:** Merchant Center shows 216 ineligible products: 110 with 'page unavailable' (404) and 70 with promotional overlay violations. At least ₹80,285 was spent on affected products with zero conversions (for example, one serum SKU spent ₹1,614 on 480 clicks into a 404 page). Active products must have URLs repaired in the Shopify feed.
* **4. Frequent budget changes:** Daily budgets were changed 140 times in under 60 days (74 increases, 66 decreases). Constant changes make Smart Bidding performance harder to read and slower to stabilize.
* **5. Off-category search queries:** A campaign named 'Search-Brand' used broad match and triggered queries unrelated to beauty (for example, gym equipment and a badminton ankle support). The spend on these queries is not yet quantified; it will be after a full search terms export.
* **6. Merchant Center catalog distribution & high-click SKU forensics:** Official Merchant Center performance exports (Sep 1 – Nov 19) across 3,411 SKUs show 6.14 Crore impressions and 6,29,968 clicks. The 48.77 tracked purchases reflect an unlinked free/organic listing tracking artifact currently under technical review, whereas Google Ads and Meta recorded 7,298 conversions in October and Shopify confirmed 9,143 store orders. Crucially, 10 of the top 15 SKUs by clicks are high_28plus hero products (e.g. Floractive Nanoplastia ₹5,500, Loreal Absolut Repair). They must NOT be blanket-excluded. Exclusions are strictly restricted to negative unit margin items (standalone ₹69 single sachets where ~₹45 shipping exceeds ₹37 gross margin) and EXCLUDE_lowmargin (<15%) tags (Exhibit G).

## 4. Meta Ads Audit & 5. Avoidable Spend

Meta spend during the interim period totaled ₹32.99 Lakhs.
* **1. Retargeting more expensive than prospecting:** 'Retargeting — DPA - Fabb6' spent ₹9.50 Lakhs for 1,850 purchases at ₹513.70 CPA (peaking at ₹570.31 in October), against ₹479.53 for cold prospecting, with frequency of 3.21 and no past-purchaser exclusions. Excess cost against the cold-prospecting CPA is about ₹63,000.
* **2. Overlapping Advantage+ Shopping campaigns:** Three Advantage+ Shopping campaigns ran at the same time without product exclusions, so they competed in the same auctions. Account CPM rose from ₹56.15 in August to ₹104.17 in October (+85%) and ₹137.03 in November (+144%). Consolidation to one master Advantage+ campaign will eliminate auction overlap.
* **3. 'IG-Traffic' link-click campaign:** ₹50,214 across 8.32 million impressions produced 1,02,322 link clicks, 28 landing page views and 0 purchases (99.97% drop-off between click and page view).
* **4. App install campaigns:** The iOS campaign spent ₹19,161 at frequency 13.18 with 0 sales. The Android campaign spent about ₹1.38 Lakhs at ₹1,082 CPA.
* **5. Click-to-landing-page gap:** Meta reported 7,34,064 link clicks but only 3,01,798 landing page views on Shopify: a 58.9% gap (52.2% excluding IG-Traffic). Likely causes are mobile page speed, accidental clicks, and pixel or Conversions API (CAPI) misfires. We will diagnose these in the first two weeks.

## 6. Turnaround Plan

Admin access has been verified across Google Ads, Meta Business Suite, Google Merchant Center and Shopify. Shopify is the source of truth for revenue and orders throughout.

### Phase 1: Immediate triage (hours 0 – 48)
* Pause 'IG-Traffic' and the iOS and Android app install campaigns.
* Deploy Simprosys Google Shopping Feed app to instantly eliminate 404 broken URLs and stop ad spend on 0-stock products.
* Exclude negative unit margin SKUs (standalone ₹69 single-use sachets) and EXCLUDE_lowmargin (<15%) tags via automated feed custom labels.
* Remove foreign-brand copy from all Performance Max asset groups.
* Confirm a single primary Purchase conversion action is active in Google Ads and matches Shopify order counts. Verify Enhanced Conversions, and test Meta CAPI deduplication.

### Phase 2: Restructure and stabilize (December 2026)
* Segment Google into High Margin Heroes (60% budget), Core Volume (20%), Category Search (13%), and Brand Search (7%).
* Consolidate Meta into one master Advantage+ Shopping campaign (70% budget) with a 15% existing-customer budget cap.
* Add 30-day and 180-day purchaser exclusions to DPA (15% budget), with frequency capped at 2.2 or below.
* Deploy GoKwik (https://www.gokwik.co/) on Shopify for 1-click checkout, automated abandoned cart recovery, and COD RTO risk reduction, alongside automated WhatsApp (Interakt/Wati) and email replenishment workflows timed to 30, 45, and 60 days (~₹35k–₹50k/mo software cost, zero incremental media spend).

### Phase 3: Profitable scaling (January – February 2027)
* Increase budgets by no more than 20% every 72 hours per campaign.
* Expand high-intent non-brand Google search for hero categories (serums, brightening, anti-aging).
* Scale automated replenishment CRM flows to capture high-margin repeat replenishment revenue.

**Scaling decision rule:** scale a step only if trailing 7-day confirmed Gross ROAS is at least 2.50x and CPA is within target. Hold spend between 2.30x and 2.50x. Roll back the last increase if Gross ROAS stays below 2.30x for 7 consecutive days. All planned budget expansions in Section 7 are strictly conditional on maintaining this performance threshold.

## 8. Operating Guardrails

* **1. Paused-first creation:** New campaigns, ad sets and creatives are created paused and go live only after tracking, UTM parameters and margin review are confirmed.
* **2. Budget pacing:** Increases are capped at 20% every 72 hours per campaign.
* **3. Audience hygiene:** Prospecting campaigns exclude 30-day purchasers and customer lists. Retargeting excludes purchasers.
* **4. Daily feed checks:** Automated real-time scans via Simprosys Google Shopping Feed immediately suppress products with dead URLs or zero inventory.
* **5. Reporting cadence:** A daily pacing update by WhatsApp or Slack, a weekly performance review, and a monthly reconciliation against Shopify net revenue.

## Appendix B: Definitions

* **Shopify Confirmed Gross Sales:** Confirmed orders from Shopify before deductions. Primary planning and weekly performance gating metric.
* **Total Sales Revenue (Net):** Actual net recognized sales revenue from Shopify (after discounts and returns, including taxes and shipping). Averages ~80% of gross sales across baseline.
* **Confirmed Gross ROAS:** Shopify Confirmed Gross Sales divided by total ad spend. Account targets: 3.46x Dec, 3.79x Jan, 4.35x Feb (3.91x Q1 blended).
* **Spend per Store Order (Blended CPA):** Total ad spend divided by total Shopify store orders (₹293 Dec, ₹291 Jan, ₹283 Feb; Q1 avg ₹288.53). Primary governing business unit metric.
* **Paid orders and platform CPA:** Ad-attributed orders and platform-specific acquisition cost. Target improves by 4% monthly (Google ₹450 → ₹410, Meta ₹450 → ₹385).
* **November to date:** 1 – 18 November 2026 (18 days), extrapolated to 30 days for reference run-rate comparison.
