Paid CPA Inflation Trend (Dec 2025 - Nov 2026)

1. The "Glam+" Copy-Paste Disaster (Catastrophic Negligence)

In multiple live Google Performance Max campaigns (T4 Pro/Salon, T3 Premium, Pilgrim, YS24), competitor agency SilverXis literally copy-pasted headlines and copy from another client named "Glam+": "Beauty & Skincare Must-Haves, Glam+ Store", "Glow Up with Glam+ | Flas Sale is Live" (with typos!), and audience signals tagged "Glam Plus - Custom -CWV". FABB6 spent real advertising rupees promoting a completely different brand!

2. The September 18 Tracking Blackout (Google Ads ₹7,569 CPA)

On Sep 18, 2026, the competitor executed Conversion "Purchase-2026" removed in Google Ads. This completely blinded Google's Smart Bidding algorithms. Consequently, in September, Google Ads delivered only 42 purchases on ₹3.18 Lakhs spend, resulting in an absurd CPA of ₹7,568.93 and a ROAS of 0.15x.

3. October Festive Season (Diwali) Meltdown

During the highest e-commerce demand period in India, SilverXis pumped ₹42.47 Lakhs into ads. But Meta CPA surged to an all-time record high of ₹532.42 and ROAS tanked to 2.47x. Despite heavy spending, paid purchases (7,298) were lower than unoptimized July actuals (8,313 purchases on ₹43.6L spend).

4. Retargeting Cost Inversion (DPA Higher CPA than Prospecting)

Dynamic Product Ads (Retargeting — DPA) spent ₹9,43,971 at a CPA of ₹514.99 (peaking at ₹570.31 in October). Retargeting was actually more expensive than cold prospecting because of severe audience exhaustion and no frequency capping.

5. ₹80,285 Burned on 110 Dead Landing Pages (404 Errors)

Techno Exponent warned FABB6 in August about 186 Merchant Center catalog errors. The competitor ignored it. 110 products continued sending paid traffic to "Product page unavailable" 404 links (e.g. Foxtale 5% Niacinamide Serum spent ₹1,614 across 480 clicks with 0 sales).

6. Zero-Conversion Traffic Waste & Zombie App Ads

Burned ₹50,214 on IG-Traffic for 8.31M impressions and exactly 0 purchases. Simultaneously burned ₹1.58 Lakhs on app promotion ads bombarding users at an extreme frequency of 13.18 at CPAs over ₹1,080.

7. 140+ Hyperactive Budget Changes (Permanent Learning Phase)

SilverXis tweaked campaign budgets 140 times (74 increases, 66 decreases), often adjusting budgets by minor amounts every few hours/days. This constantly reset machine learning in Google Ads and Meta Ads, destroying bid stability.
