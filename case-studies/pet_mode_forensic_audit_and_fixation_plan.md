## 4. Forensic Google Ads Audit Findings

### 4.1 Google Merchant Center Feed Hygiene & "Limited" Status Resolution
In Google Merchant Center, product listings are currently flagged with yellow **"Limited"** eligibility status.
* **Root Cause**: In Shopify's Google & YouTube app, multi-country publishing was enabled across international markets where local shipping rates have not been configured, generating thousands of unserviced variant entries and *"Missing shipping info in some countries"* warnings.
* **Impact**: Google Shopping algorithms prioritize clean feeds with complete, localized shipping data. Unserviced foreign feed rows fragment crawl budget and dilute account-level trust scores.
* **Fixation Action**: In Shopify Admin > Apps > Google & YouTube > Settings, restrict targeted country availability strictly to **United Kingdom (GBP)**. This purges unserviced variant rows, resolves "Limited" warnings, and converts your product feed to green **"Approved"** status.

---

### 4.2 Wasted Search Terms in Performance Max (£432.60 / 42.7% of Google Spend)
The active campaign (`Sales-Performance Max-15.5.26`) has spent £981.40 for 24 conversions (CPA £40.89). However, over **£430+** was drained on non-converting search queries:
* **Competitor Brand Queries**: `costco poo bags` (Costco), `gulpup` (Direct competitor), `3 peaks running dog lead` (Pets at Home brand).
* **Controversial Queries**: `barking shock collar`, `dog shock collar` (Pet Mode sells humane smart automatic sound/vibration collars, not shock collars; shoppers bounce immediately).
* **Vague Search Terms**: `dog cooling mat`, `dog water bottle` (clicked without purchase intent).

> [!CAUTION]
> **Action Required**: Add an account-level negative keyword list containing competitor brand names, controversial terms (`shock`, `electric`), and bargain-seeker terms (`free`, `cheap`, `wholesale`).
