# Content Publishing Directive (v2)

## Objective
Write, format, and publish high-quality, expert-level, and search-compliant e-commerce and paid media articles. All posts must align with Google's E-E-A-T guidelines, Helpful Content standards, and our SEO blog writing framework — while reading like they were written by a specific human expert, not a template.

**What changed in v2:** Section 0 (new) fixes the root cause of repetitive, AI-fingerprinted openings across posts. Section 3's template has been rewritten to stop encoding a single formula. Section 4 adds a duplicate-content gate before anything gets scheduled.

---

## 0. Anti-AI-Pattern / Editorial Variety Rule (READ FIRST)

This section exists because prior output converged on one rhetorical skeleton across dozens of posts:

> *"Are you running [X], but noticing [Y]? In [Z] marketing, choosing between [A] and [B] dictates... In this comprehensive guide, you will master..."*

That pattern is easy for both readers and Google's helpful-content systems to recognize as templated, and it flattens 75 different topics into one voice. The instruction "first 100 words must contain the primary keyword, state the user's problem, and promise a solution" is still correct — but it must never be satisfied the same way twice in a row.

### Banned as opening sentences (do not use in the first paragraph of any post)
- "Are you running/looking for/wondering..."
- "Have you ever..."
- "In [modern/today's/2026's] [X] marketing..."
- "In this comprehensive/definitive/ultimate guide, you will..."
- "Imagine you are..."
- Any rhetorical question as the literal first sentence

### Required: rotate the lead-in type
Before drafting, pick ONE opening style from this list, and do not reuse the same style two posts in a row (check the last 3 published posts' openings before choosing):

1. **Direct-answer lead** — answer the query in sentence one, expand after. ("A modular kitchen with an L-shaped layout costs ₹1.5–3 lakhs in most Indian metros, depending on...")
2. **Data/stat-led** — open with a specific number, benchmark, or finding. ("Average CPCs on branded search terms rose 22% between Q1 and Q3 2026 across the accounts we manage...")
3. **Scenario/case lead** — a concrete, specific situation (not a hypothetical "you"), drawn from real client work where possible. ("One of our construction clients was paying ₹450 per lead from a single misconfigured match-type setting.")
4. **Definitional lead** — for informational-intent queries only. State what the thing is, then why it matters now.
5. **Contrarian/myth-correction lead** — open by naming a common misconception, then correct it.
6. **News-hook lead** — tie to a recent platform change, policy update, or industry event, with a date.

### Additional variety controls
- Vary sentence length and paragraph length within a post — don't let every section open with a rhetorical question either.
- Vary section transitions. Don't default every H2 to "Understanding [Topic]" or "How to [Topic]."
- Author's voice should carry a recognizable point of view or opinion at least once per post (per E-E-A-T's "experience" signal) — a specific number from a real account, a specific mistake seen, a specific recommendation the author would defend.
- Before submitting a draft, self-check it against the last 5 published posts for repeated opening structures, repeated stock phrases ("represents a critical," "represents a reliable path to"), and repeated closing-paragraph templates. If 2+ matches are found, rewrite.

## 1. The SEO Blog Writing Framework

### Phase 1: Keyword Research & Intent Mapping
*   **Primary Keyword:** Target exactly one primary keyword.
*   **Secondary Keywords:** Map 5–15 related secondary keywords and multiple long-tail keywords.
*   **Search Intent Analysis:** Categorize search intent before writing:
    *   *Informational:* "What is a modular kitchen"
    *   *Commercial:* "Best modular kitchen design" (never write a commercial article like an informational article)
    *   *Transactional:* "Modular kitchen cost in Delhi"
    *   *Navigational:* "IKEA modular kitchen"

### Phase 2: Competitor Analysis
*   Search the target keyword on Google.
*   Analyze the Top 10 results for word count, headings, images, FAQs, internal links, external links, content depth, and missing topics.
*   **Goal:** Create an article **20–30% better** than the best-ranking competitor by addressing all gaps and providing more comprehensive, actionable solutions — not just a longer version of the same structure.

### Phase 3: SEO Content Structure
*   **URL Slug:** Short, clean, lowercase, primary keyword included, no dates or years, no stop words.
*   **Title Tag:** (50–60 characters) CTR-focused, primary keyword near the start + benefit + power word.
*   **Meta Description:** (140–160 characters) Primary keyword + value proposition + CTA.
*   **H1 Optimization:** Only ONE H1 (the post title, managed by the theme). Do not duplicate H1 in the content body.
*   **H2 Structure:** Every `<h2>` targets a secondary keyword.
*   **Introduction:** First 100 words contain the primary keyword, state the problem, and promise a solution — using one of the six lead-in styles in Section 0, chosen deliberately, not defaulted.
*   **Body Content Flow:** Problem ➔ Explanation ➔ Solution ➔ Example ➔ Expert Tip — but vary how each transition is phrased between posts.
*   **Content Depth:**
    *   *Low competition:* 1,200+ words
    *   *Medium competition:* 2,000+ words
    *   *High competition:* 3,000–5,000+ words
*   **Keyword Optimization:** Density target 0.8–1.5% for the primary keyword. Integrate related semantic/LSI entities naturally.
*   **Internal Linking:** 5–15 internal links (roughly 1 per 400–600 words) to related posts, category, or service pages.
*   **External Linking:** 2–5 high-quality external links to authoritative sources (.gov, .edu, official platform documentation, industry reports).
*   **Image SEO:** Custom graphics/charts with descriptive filenames (e.g., `modular-kitchen-design.webp`) and descriptive alt text.
*   **FAQ Section:** 5–10 FAQs at the end, each answered directly in 40–60 words for Featured Snippets.
*   **E-E-A-T & AI Content Rules:** Every post needs at least one first-hand detail (a real number, account, or client scenario — anonymized as needed), a named author with a visible bio/credential, and at least one original opinion or recommendation the author would stand behind. AI-assisted drafts must go through human editing that adds these elements, not just a grammar pass.

## 2. Advanced Ranking Checklist
*   **Schema Markup:** Article, FAQ, Breadcrumb, and Author schema on all posts.
*   **Table of Contents (TOC):** Below the introduction, for UX and jump-link snippets.
*   **Content Freshness:** Review and update posts every 3 months in highly competitive niches, every 6 months otherwise. Log the update date and what changed.
*   **Topical Clusters:** Connect a pillar page to supporting sub-pages (e.g., "Modular Kitchen" ↔ "Kitchen Cost," "Kitchen Layouts").

## 4. Pre-Publish Checklist

Before running the WordPress publishing script, check off every item:

**Content quality & originality**
- [ ] Opening paragraph uses a lead-in style from Section 0, and it's not the same style as the previous 1–2 published posts.
- [ ] No banned opening phrases present ("Are you...", "Have you ever...", "In modern marketing...", "In this comprehensive guide...").
- [ ] At least one first-hand detail / specific number / named scenario included.
- [ ] Checked against the site's existing posts for topic and title overlap — **no duplicate or near-duplicate article exists** (search the blog by primary keyword and by working title before publishing; if an old post covers the same angle, update/merge it instead of publishing a new one).

**Technical SEO**
- [ ] URL: short, clean, lowercase, contains primary keyword, no stop words.
- [ ] Title tag: under 60 characters, primary keyword near the start, CTR-focused.
- [ ] Meta description: under 160 characters, contains primary keyword, action-oriented.
- [ ] Headings: one H1 only; multiple H2s targeting secondary keywords.
- [ ] Keyword density: 0.8–1.5% for the primary keyword, no stuffing.
- [ ] Images: compressed, alt text added, descriptive filenames.
- [ ] Featured image present (generate + upload if featured media ID is 0).
- [ ] Links: 5–15 internal links, 2–5 authoritative external links.
- [ ] Schema prepared: Article, FAQ, Breadcrumb, Author.
- [ ] Mobile-friendly rendering checked.

**Author & trust signals**
- [ ] Author byline and bio link present and correct.
- [ ] Any claims sourced or attributable to real account/campaign data noted internally (even if anonymized in the post).
