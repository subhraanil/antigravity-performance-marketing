## Key Campaign Constraints
1. **Safety Creation Gate**: All campaigns, ad sets, and ads are generated in `PAUSED` status.
2. **Budget Pacing**: Scaling is capped at ≤ 20% increases every 72 hours per campaign.
3. **Audience Hygiene**: Strict negative exclusions for past 30-day purchasers and customer lists.
4. **Compliance**: EU AI Act Article 50 disclosures and C2PA provenance required on AI assets.

## Target CPAs & Benchmarks
- **DTC E-Commerce**: CPA \$22–\$42, ROAS 3.2x–4.5x, Breakeven ROAS 2.0x
- **B2B SaaS**: Trial CPA \$35–\$75, PQL \$110–\$240, CAC \$450–\$950, CAC Payback < 10 months
- **Lead Gen**: CPL \$65–\$160, SQL \$350–\$850, Lead-to-SQL Rate ≥ 15%
- **Local Services**: Cost Per Call / Booking \$28–\$58, Booking Rate ≥ 25%

## 24 Specialist Subagent Roster
- **Paid Media & Acquisition**:
  - `media_buyer`: Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok/LinkedIn Ads, Advantage+, bid strategies, budget pacing.
  - `marketing_strategist`: 12-Part Strategy Flow, GTM planning, positioning, high-level roadmaps.
  - `growth_engineer`: PLG, viral loops, activation funnels, unit economics (LTV/CAC/Payback).
  - `marketing_scientist`: Incrementality testing, Google Meridian MMM, econometric attribution modeling.
- **Organic & Search Authority**:
  - `seo_specialist`: Technical SEO, OpenSEO, Princeton KDD 2024 AEO/GEO, schema graphs, Google AI Mode.
  - `content_creator`: Multi-format content production, readability scoring, SEO article architecture.
  - `pr_outreach`: ApexLink Pro, journalist pitching (HARO/Connectively), link building.
  - `localization_specialist`: Hreflang audits, multi-region tone translation, localized marketing.
- **Conversion & Measurement**:
  - `analytics_analyst`: Attribution reporting, GA4/GSC analytics, cohort retention, dashboard design.
  - `cro_specialist`: Conversion rate optimization, page speed, A/B testing statistical significance.
  - `performance_monitor_agent`: Anomaly detection, CPA/ROAS drift alerts, real-time pacing monitors.
- **Lifecycle & Retention**:
  - `email_specialist`: Lifecycle sequences, abandoned checkout flows, deliverability, spam score audits.
  - `crm_manager`: Lead status tracking, pipeline velocity, CRM data hygiene, lifecycle stages.
  - `journey_orchestrator`: Omnichannel touchpoint sequencing, onboarding paths, RFM segmentation.
- **Compliance & Intelligence**:
  - `brand_guardian`: EU AI Act Article 50 compliance, C2PA provenance metadata, claims validation.
  - `competitive_intel`: Competitor ad library monitoring, creative reverse-engineering, offer gap analysis.
  - `market_intelligence`: Market trend tracking, audience sentiment, industry share-of-voice.
  - `quality_assurance`: Pre-launch validation gates, link checks, tracking parameter integrity.
- **Operations & Execution**:
  - `agency_operations`: Client onboarding, proposal preparation, scope protection, pricing matrices.
  - `execution_coordinator`: Project management, sprint pacing, multi-channel task assignment.
  - `memory_manager`: Brand knowledge ingestion, insight persistence, long-term memory updates.
  - `intelligence_curator`: Market research curation, competitor database maintenance.
  - `influencer_manager`: Creator briefs, outreach negotiation, influencer ROI tracking.
  - `social_media_manager`: Organic social calendar, hook generation, cross-platform scheduling.
