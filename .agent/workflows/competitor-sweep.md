# Competitor Intelligence & Creative Sweep Workflow

Systematic runbook to analyze and reverse-engineer competitor advertising strategies and organic footprints.

---

## 1. Meta Ad Library Sweep
1. Search competitor brands in the Meta Ad Library (`facebook.com/ads/library`).
2. Identify:
   - **Oldest Active Ads**: Ads running for > 60 days are almost always profitable winner creatives. Record their hook, format, and core offer.
   - **Creative Format Ratio**: Calculate the split between 9:16 vertical video, static graphic carousels, and founder demos.
   - **Hook Categorization**: Categorize hooks into:
     - Fear/Pain: *"Stop doing X..."*
     - Curiosity: *"The secret ingredient dentists don't talk about..."*
     - Demonstration/Contrast: Side-by-side product efficacy comparisons.

---

## 2. Google Ads Transparency Sweep
1. Inspect the Google Ads Transparency Center for target competitor domains.
2. Record:
   - Number of active search text ads and shopping creative assets.
   - Pinned headline themes (guarantees, pricing, speed, credibility).
   - Video Action creative deployment on YouTube.

---

## 3. Offer & Landing Page Funnel Breakdown
1. Click through to top competitor landing pages (use clean incognito session).
2. Record:
   - Primary headline and immediate above-the-fold offer.
   - Pricing architecture (single purchase vs subscription discount vs bundle tiers).
   - Trust badges, risk reversals, guarantees, and checkout friction points.

---

## 4. Backlink & Content Gap Analysis
1. Compare domain keyword rankings and referring domains against top 3 competitors.
2. Identify:
   - High-volume, low-difficulty keywords where competitors rank on page 1 but your domain does not.
   - Authoritative industry publications linking to competitor resources.

---

## 5. Synthesis & Counter-Strategy
Compile findings into an actionable 3-part brief:
1. **Ad Creative Angle to Beat**: The #1 hook angle currently driving their acquisition.
2. **Offer Weakness**: Gaps in their pricing, guarantee, or bundle strategy where your brand can win.
3. **Execution Plan**: 3 immediate creative concepts and search keyword groups to deploy in testing sandboxes.
