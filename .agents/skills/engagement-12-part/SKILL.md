# 12-Part Digital Marketing Strategy Engagement Workflow
# Source: digital-marketing-pro framework

Execute the canonical 12-part strategy flow to audit, position, and scale any brand.

---

## Phase 1: Intake, Discovery & Market Research

### 1. Rapid Intake & Discovery Checklist
Ask the client / stakeholder for key operational inputs:
- **Website URL**: Scan existing value proposition and site architecture using `read_browser_page`.
- **Primary Business Objectives**: Quantified targets (Lead Generation, CAC reduction, Revenue scaling, App installs).
- **Target Audience & ICP**: Demographics, firmographics, key pain points, and alternatives used.
- **Top Competitors**: Main direct rivals and aspirational category leaders.
- **Budget & Timeline**: Monthly media budget thresholds and target payback window.

### 2. Part 1 — Client Intake (Stone vs. Opinion)
- Separate verifiable facts (**Stone**: past revenue, verified CPA, margin data, active geographies) from assumptions (**Opinion**: "Our audience loves X", "Competitor Y is cheaper").

### 3. Part 2 — External Market Research
- Independent market sizing, industry benchmark queries, macro trends without client preconceptions.

---

## Phase 2: Core Strategic Foundation

### 4. Part 3 — Four Core Documents (61 Steps)
- **3.1 Business & SBU Analysis**: Unit economics, contribution margins, LTV/CAC ratios.
- **3.2 Segmentation Framework**: Actionable persona definitions, TAM/SAM/SOM breakdown.
- **3.3 Brand Positioning & Communications**: Differentiation statement, tone of voice, proof points.
- **3.4 DMFlow (Digital Marketing Flow)**: Customer journey mapping from first touch to retention.

### 5. Part 4 — Competitive & Audience Deep Dive
- Competitor ad monitoring (Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency), keyword share of voice, creative gap analysis.

---

## Phase 3: Validation & Recalibration

### 6. Part 5 — Client Validation Gate
- Deliver Client Validation Document; client marks findings as Accept / Reject / Edit.

### 7. Part 6 — Selective v2 Re-runs
- Apply Decision Matrix to re-run only invalidated downstream documents. Maintain v1 (unbiased market reality) and v2 (client-reconciled operating reality).

---

## Phase 4: Operating Architecture & Execution

### 8. Part 7 — Preparation Layer
- Campaign architecture, KPI tree, content pillars, approval matrices.

### 9. Part 8 — Growth Plan & 12-Month Yearly Planner
- Flagship 11-section strategy document, channel budget splits, milestone timeline.

### 10. Part 9 — Channel Strategy Fan-Out
- Detailed operational plans for Paid Social (Meta, TikTok), Paid Search (Google), SEO & AEO, Lifecycle Email, and Influencers.

### 11. Part 10 — Execution Artefacts
- High-converting ad copy (Hook-Hold-Offer), RSA copy matrices, email sequences, landing page briefs.

### 12. Part 11 — Creative Briefs (AI & Human Production)
- Visual storyboards, EU AI Act Article 50 & C2PA disclosure tags, model specs (Gemini Omni, Imagen 3, Veo 3.1).

### 13. Part 12 — Continuous Improvement Loop
- Quarterly retrospective, media signals fed back into positioning, creative production, and product offering adjustments.
