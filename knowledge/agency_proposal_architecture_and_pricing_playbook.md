# Agency Digital Marketing Proposal Architecture & Pricing Playbook

## Executive Overview

Winning enterprise and high-growth SME digital marketing retainers requires proposals that transcend generic agency slide decks. This playbook operationalizes an **evidence-based, financially modeled, and legally guardrailed proposal preparation standard**.

Every proposal produced under this framework is:
1. **Rooted in Verified Data:** Verified client digital assets, live keyword intent, and competitor search footprints.
2. **Financially Modeled:** Supported by 3-tier mathematical forecasting (**Conservative, Expected, Aggressive**) with explicit CPL, CPA, and ROAS scenarios.
3. **Dual Format Delivery:** Interactive HTML Web Dashboard (responsive layout, SVG gauges, sticky navigation) and CMMI-Level Executive Deck (DOCX/PDF).
4. **Scope-Protected:** Clear deliverable boundaries, billing protocols, and discontinuation policies that prevent scope creep.

## 3. Financial & Conversion Modeling Architecture

Never promise arbitrary numbers. Ground all forecasts in the **Three-Scenario Probability Model**:

### Mathematical Forecasting Engine:
1. **Projected Clicks:** $\text{Clicks} = \frac{\text{Monthly Media Budget}}{\text{Blended CPC}}$
2. **Projected Inquiries / Leads:** $\text{Leads} = \text{Clicks} \times \text{Landing Page Conversion Rate (CVR)}$
3. **Projected Sales / Clients:** $\text{Sales} = \text{Leads} \times \text{Sales Close Rate}$
4. **Projected Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):** $\text{ROAS} = \frac{\text{Sales} \times \text{Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)}}{\text{Monthly Media Budget}}$

### The 3-Tier Scenario Standard:
- **Conservative (Worst-Case Grounding):** Assumes baseline industry low conversion rates and higher auction CPCs. Proves profitability or breakeven even under headwinds.
- **Expected (Realistic Benchmark):** Assumes proper agency execution, optimized landing pages, and standard conversion rates.
- **Aggressive (Scale & Optimization Target):** Assumes high creative resonance, above-average landing page conversion, and compound retargeting efficiency.

## 4. Scope Boundaries & Legal Guardrails

Every agency proposal must explicitly state what is included and what requires change-order authorization:

### Mandatory Scope Boundaries:
- **Media Spend Responsibility:** All ad spend is paid directly by the client to advertising platforms (Google, Meta, LinkedIn). Agency retainers cover strategic management, execution, and reporting only.
- **Creative Production Caps:** Explicitly state the number of ad creative assets included per month (e.g. 8 static graphics + 2 video variations). Additional assets billed at fixed rate.
- **Approval Checkpoints:** No campaign may go live without formal client sign-off on ad copy, target audiences, and landing page URLs.
- **Cancellation SLA:** Standard 30-day written notice following completion of the initial 90-day foundation commitment.
