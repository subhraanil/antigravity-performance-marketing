# Agency Digital Marketing Proposal Architecture & Pricing Playbook
# TechnoExponent Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) | Version 2.0

This playbook operationalizes the standardized, evidence-based methodology for researching, structuring, modeling, and generating high-converting digital marketing proposals at TechnoExponent.

---

## 1. Executive Overview & Core Philosophy

Winning enterprise and high-growth SME digital marketing retainers requires proposals that transcend generic agency slide decks. Every proposal produced under this framework is:
- **Evidence-Based**: Rooted in verifiable client audit data, real competitor gaps, and historical performance benchmarks.
- **Financially Modeled**: Contains dynamic scenario modeling (Conservative, Expected, Aggressive) demonstrating clear CAC, ROAS, and ROI payback.
- **Legally Guardrailed**: Transparent scope of work, defined deliverables, and robust terms and conditions (T&Cs).

## 3. Financial Modeling & Revenue Projections

Proposals must include a 3-tier financial model to establish realistic expectations:

$$\text{Blended MER} = \frac{\text{Total Gross Revenue}}{\text{Total Media Spend}}$$

$$\text{Payback Period (Months)} = \frac{\text{Fully Loaded CAC}}{\text{Monthly Gross Profit Per Customer}}$$

### Scenario Matrix Template
- **Conservative (Worst-Case)**: CPA $+25\%$, Conversion Rate $-20\%$. Validates breakeven viability.
- **Expected (Base-Case)**: Industry median benchmarks (DTC CPA $22–$42, Lead Gen CPL $65–$160).
- **Aggressive (Scale-Case)**: Creative winner breakout, CPA $-20\%$, volume $+50\%$.

## 4. Proposal Design Tokens & Responsive Dashboard Architecture

### Responsive HTML Dashboard Standard
For digital presentations, deliver proposals using the **Interactive Dashboard Standard**:
- **Layout**: 100% responsive width grid featuring a 280px sticky sidebar navigation on desktop paired with a modular card content area.
- **Brand Tokens**:
  - Primary Highlight: **TechnoExponent Blue** (`#2361a8`)
  - Accent / Vibrant: **Cyan** (`#00aeef`)
  - Background Surfaces: Clean Slate & White (`#f8fafc`, `#ffffff`)
- **Interactive Visuals**: Embedded CSS/SVG gauges for PageSpeed and conversion forecasts.
- **Delivery**: Self-contained single `.html` document (embedded styles, zero external script dependencies, instant offline browser preview).

## 6. Proposal Preparation Checklist (Sign-Off Gate)

Before delivering any proposal to client leadership, verify:
- [ ] **No Speculative Assumptions**: All audit metrics cite actual observed data.
- [ ] **Scope Separation**: Clear boundaries between marketing deliverables (leads/traffic) and client closing.
- [ ] **Financial Sanity Check**: Unit economics and breakeven ROAS are mathematically sound.
- [ ] **Brand Tokens Applied**: Primary `#2361a8` and Cyan `#00aeef` used consistently.
- [ ] **Clickable Deliverables**: All referenced reports link using `file://` hyperlinks.
