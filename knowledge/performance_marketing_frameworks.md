# Performance Marketing Frameworks & Strategy Flow
# Source: digital-marketing-pro & Industry Standards

## 1. The Canonical 12-Part Strategy Flow
- **Part 1 — Client Inputs**: Separate verifiable empirical data ("Stone": verified revenue, margin, CPA) from subjective client beliefs ("Opinion": audience preferences, competitor assumptions).
- **Part 2 — External Research**: Unbiased market sizing, TAM/SAM/SOM, and competitive research conducted independently of client narrative.
- **Part 3 — Four Core Documents (61 Steps)**:
  - 3.1 Business & SBU Analysis: Unit economics, margins, LTV/CAC, contribution margin.
  - 3.2 Segmentation Framework: Actionable persona definitions, buying triggers.
  - 3.3 Brand Positioning & Communications: Differentiation hook, brand pillars, tone.
  - 3.4 DMFlow: Customer acquisition journey mapping across all stages.
- **Part 4 — Competitive, Customer & Market Analysis**: Deep-dive into competitor creative libraries, keywords, and market share.
- **Part 5 — Client Validation Document**: Formal client review gate (Accept, Reject, Edit).
- **Part 6 — Selective v2 Re-runs**: Re-running affected downstream documents via the Decision Matrix.
- **Part 7 — Preparation Documents**: Campaign architecture, KPI hierarchy, approval chains.
- **Part 8 — Growth Plan & 12-Month Yearly Planner**: Flagship roadmap with monthly budget allocations.
- **Part 9 — Channel Strategy Fan-Out**: Dedicated channel playbooks (Meta, Google, TikTok, Email, SEO).
- **Part 10 — Execution Artefacts**: Ad copy, headlines, CTAs, email sequences.
- **Part 11 — Creative Briefs**: Storyboards, format specs, C2PA & EU AI Act Article 50 compliance.
- **Part 12 — Continuous Improvement Loop**: Quarterly performance loops feeding product & pricing adjustments.

## 2. Core Architectural Principles
- **Two-Views Model**: Maintain v1 (unbiased market reality) and v2 (client-reconciled operational reality) side-by-side. Never discard v1.
- **70/20/10 Budget Rule**:
  - 70% to proven, scalable revenue engines (CBO/ASC/PMax).
  - 20% to iterative testing sandboxes (ABO creative testing).
  - 10% to moonshot experimental channels or radical concepts.
