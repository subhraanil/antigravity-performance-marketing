## 2. Unit Economic Equations
- **Blended CAC**: Total Paid Ad Spend / Total New Acquired Customers
- **Target CPA**: (AOV x Gross Margin %) - Target Contribution Margin %
- **Breakeven ROAS**: 1 / Gross Margin %
- **K-Factor (Virality)**: Invites Sent per User x Conversion Rate of Invites (Target K > 0.3)
