# Advertising Compliance, Governance & EU AI Act (Article 50)

## 1. EU AI Act Article 50 (Mandatory for AI Media)
- **C2PA Provenance Metadata**: All AI-generated or AI-altered images, audio, and video deployed in campaigns targeting EU audiences must be signed with C2PA metadata.
- **Deepfake & Avatar Disclosure**: Any synthetic human likeness, digital spokesperson, or AI voiceover must feature a conspicuous on-screen visual disclaimer ("Created with AI") in the first 3 seconds and in the video description.

## 2. Regulatory & Privacy Guardrails
- **Signal Resilience**: Rely on server-side tracking (Meta Conversions API via Gateway or server GTM, Google Enhanced Conversions) rather than third-party browser cookies alone.
- **GDPR / CCPA / DPDPA Compliance**: Always honor user consent flags; never ingest PII directly into unhashed custom audiences.
- **Substantiation of Claims**: Every numerical benefit or guarantee must have verifiable customer or clinical proof prior to ad launch.
